Aureum is a concierge operations platform operated by Citadel Software Solutions ("Citadel", "we"). This notice explains how personal data is handled on the platform. It covers two very different groups of people: the hotel staff who use Aureum, and the hotel guests whose details staff record while doing their jobs.

Hotel staff accounts

For staff accounts, Citadel is the data controller. When your hotel creates an account for you we hold:

  • Your name, username and work email address
  • Your password, stored only as a secure hash
  • The hotel you work for, and the roles you hold there
  • A record of your sign-ins and of when you viewed modules containing guest details

We process this data to operate your account (performance of a contract) and to keep an audit trail of access to guest data (legitimate interest in the security of the platform). Access records are kept for 12 months and then deleted automatically.

When you leave the hotel, your login account is deleted. Your name remains attached to past records you created — packages you logged, bookings you made — because the hotel needs its operational history to stay attributable.

Hotel guests

Details about guests — names, contact details, bookings, deliveries, lost property — are recorded by hotel staff on the hotel's instructions. For this data your hotel is the data controller and Citadel is its processor. The hotel decides what is recorded and how long it is kept; Citadel stores it, secures it, and deletes or anonymises it on the hotel's schedule.

If you are a guest and want to know what a hotel holds about you, or want it corrected or deleted, contact the hotel directly. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to the hotel and support them in answering it.

Where data is stored

The platform is hosted on Amazon Web Services. The current hosting region and the full list of service providers that handle data on our behalf are published on our sub-processors page, which is kept up to date as providers change.

How long we keep data

  • Staff accounts: for as long as the account is active; deleted at offboarding.
  • Guest records: each hotel sets a retention period per module; once it passes, guest identifiers are removed automatically and only anonymous operational records remain.
  • Access logs: 12 months.

Your rights

Under data protection law you can ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, ask us to restrict or object to processing, and complain to a supervisory authority (in the UK, the ICO). For staff data, contact us at the address below. For guest data, contact your hotel, which controls that data.
